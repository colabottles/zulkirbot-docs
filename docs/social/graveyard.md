# The Graveyard

## !graveyard

The hall of fallen heroes. View adventurers who have met their end in the dungeon. Shows the last 5 adventurers to fall.

Shortcut keys: `grave`, `rip`, `fallen`. The graveyard tracks up to 20 heroes, ranked by XP earned.

> [!DANGER]
> Death is permanent. If your HP reaches 0 in combat, your character is gone. Use `!join` to start over.
