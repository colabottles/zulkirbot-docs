# Refinement Stones & Motes

Refinement stones and motes are upgrade currencies used by the `!upgrade` system. They cannot be spent on anything else.

## Checking Your Balance

!status materials

Output example:
> 💎 @username — Refinement Stones: 145 | Motes: 3

## Refinement Stones

Refinement stones are the primary upgrade material. They are common and earned from multiple sources.

### Refinement Stone Sources

| Source | Amount | Chance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `!salvage` any item | Scales with rarity (10–1,500) | Always |
| `!explore` | 5–15 stones | 8% per explore |
| `!weekly` | 20–50 stones | 20% per claim |

## Motes

Motes are rarer upgrade materials required at Rare → Epic and above. They cannot be obtained from common sources.

### Mote Sources

| Source | Amount | Chance/Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `!salvage` rare+ item | 1–20 (scales with rarity) | Always |
| `!explore` | 1 mote | 5% per explore |
| `!weekly` | 1–3 motes | 10% per claim |
| Standard campaign clear | 1 mote | Full clear |
| Named campaign clear | 3 motes | Full clear |
| Greyhawk Arc clear | 5 motes | Full clear |

## Tips

- Salvage duplicate common and uncommon items to build up a stone reserve before attempting rare upgrades.
- Motes are scarce — save them for items you intend to keep long-term.
- Running campaigns is the most reliable way to accumulate motes quickly.
- The `!weekly` command has a small chance of awarding both stones and motes — claim it every Monday.
