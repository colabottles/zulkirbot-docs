# Building & Upgrading

## Establishing Your Stronghold

Before you can build rooms or gather materials, you need to establish your stronghold with `!stronghold build`.

**Tier 1 cost:**

- 500gp
- 20 wood
- 10 stone

Use `!gather wood` and `!gather stone` to collect materials before building. See [Gathering Materials](/stronghold/gathering) for details.

!stronghold build

## Upgrading

Once established, use `!stronghold build` again to upgrade to the next tier. Each upgrade increases HP, ATK, DEF, and unlocks more room slots.

| Tier | Gold | Wood | Stone | Iron | Bronze | Steel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 500gp | 20 | 10 | — | — | — |
| 2 | 2,000gp | 50 | 30 | 10 | — | — |
| 3 | 8,000gp | 100 | 80 | 40 | 10 | — |
| 4 | 25,000gp | 200 | 150 | 100 | 30 | 10 |
| 5 | 75,000gp | 400 | 300 | 200 | 80 | 40 |

## Permadeath

If your character dies, your stronghold is destroyed. A flavor message is posted to chat describing its fall. All materials, rooms, and progress are lost. Use `!join` to start over and build again.
