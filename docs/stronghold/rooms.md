# Rooms

Rooms are built inside your stronghold to provide stat bonuses and special effects. Each room can only be built once. Room slots are unlocked by upgrading your stronghold tier.

## Building a Room

!build [room name]

Example:

!build barracks

## Notes

- Each room can only be built once per stronghold.
- Damaged rooms (from raids) do not provide their stat bonuses until repaired.
- Stronghold stats are recalculated automatically after each room is built or damaged.
