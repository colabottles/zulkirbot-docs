# Gathering Materials

Raw materials are required to build and upgrade your stronghold and its rooms. Use `!gather` to collect them from the surrounding area.

## Command

!gather [material]

Example:

!gather wood

## Materials

| Material | Location | Gather Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wood | Forest | 3–10 per gather |
| stone | Quarry | 3–10 per gather |
| iron | Mine | 2–8 per gather |
| bronze | Ruins | 1–6 per gather |
| steel | Forge ruins | 1–5 per gather |
| mithral | Deep caverns | 1–3 per gather |
| adamantine | Ancient mine | 1–2 per gather |

## Cooldown

There is a **60-second cooldown** between gather attempts. You cannot gather while in a fight.

## Monster Encounters

Every gather attempt has a **15% chance of provoking a monster attack**. If this triggers, a fight begins automatically. You must defeat or flee the monster before gathering again.

::: tip
Higher-tier materials (mithral, adamantine) are rare finds — gather in small amounts. Stock up on wood and stone early to get your stronghold established before pursuing rarer materials.
:::

## Checking Your Materials

Use `!stronghold` to see your current material inventory alongside your stronghold status.
