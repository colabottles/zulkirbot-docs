# Spying

Send spies to gather intelligence on another player's stronghold before deciding whether to raid.

!spy @username

## Success Chance

| Class | Success Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Rogue, Arcane Trickster | 85% |
| All other classes | 60% |

## Success

On a successful spy mission, you receive a full intel report:

- Stronghold HP and max HP
- ATK and DEF ratings
- Current morale
- All rooms (including which are damaged)
- Current material stockpile

## Failure

On failure, two outcomes are possible:

- **70% of failures** — spy slips away undetected. No intel gathered.
- **30% of failures** — spy is caught. The host is notified in chat, and you lose **10 morale**.

## Cooldown

There is a **30-second cooldown** between spy attempts.

## Example Output

> 🕵️ @username — Your spy slips in and out undetected. target's Keep: HP 180/250 | ATK 15 | DEF 18 | Morale 65 | Rooms: barracks, armory, guard_post | Materials: 45 wood, 20 stone, 8 iron

## Tips

- Spy before raiding to assess whether the target's DEF outmatches your ATK.
- High DEF + high morale = risky raid. Look for low morale targets.
- Rogues and Arcane Tricksters are significantly better spies — use them for intelligence gathering.
