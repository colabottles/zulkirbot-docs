# Steve French

Steve French is a mountain lion. He belongs to NeutralAgent. He is not available to anyone else.

## !stevefrench

NeutralAgent-only command. Summons Steve French to the dungeon.

::: warning
⚠️ Steve French does not come when called by strangers. If you are not NeutralAgent, this command will not work.
:::

## What Happens

When summoned, Steve French:

1. Arrives with flavor text drawn from Bubbles' quotes from Trailer Park Boys
2. Does something that makes everyone nearby uncomfortable in a friendly way
3. If NeutralAgent is in a fight, attacks the monster for 20–35 damage
4. Restores 15 HP to NeutralAgent from the comfort of having a mountain lion companion

## Notes

- 30 second cooldown.
- Steve French is not a hireling. He cannot die. He cannot be dismissed. He is simply Steve French.
- Combat damage is automatic — NeutralAgent does not need to do anything extra.

::: tip
ℹ️ "He's not dangerous, he's just big-boned and misunderstood." — Bubbles
:::
