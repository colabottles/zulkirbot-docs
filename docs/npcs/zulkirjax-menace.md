# Zulkir Jax — Wandering Menace

Zulkir Jax is the Lich King of Thay and the final boss of the ultimate named campaign. He also has a habit of showing up uninvited during regular play to make everyone's day slightly worse.

## How It Works

During any stream session, there is a 0.2% chance that any command triggers Zulkir Jax's appearance. He does not appear during campaigns.

When he appears he targets the player who triggered his arrival. He taunts them, applies a debuff, and waits. He will not leave until someone attacks him — or until he gets bored.

## Debuffs

Zulkir Jax applies one random debuff on appearance:

| Debuff | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Jax's Cold | -8 HP immediately |
| Jax's Doubt | -10 max HP until next rest |
| Jax's Unease | Flavor only — something feels wrong |
| Jax's Paranoia | Flavor only — every shadow looks like a phylactery |
| Jax's Mark | Flavor only — the ledger has been updated |

## Driving Him Off

Type `!attack` to drive Zulkir Jax away. He will dodge, stare at you awkwardly, and leave. One `!attack` is all it takes.

If no one attacks within 2 minutes he leaves on his own with a parting remark.

::: warning
⚠️ Zulkir Jax cannot be hurt. `!attack` drives him off but deals no damage to him.
:::

::: tip
ℹ️ Zulkir Jax does not appear during campaigns of any kind — only during regular play.
:::
