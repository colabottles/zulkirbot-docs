# Leveling

Earn XP by fighting monsters, winning duels, and exploring. The maximum level is **40**.

## Daily & Weekly Rewards

Minimum reward is 1 gp. If you roll a 1, ZulkirBot will taunt you mercilessly.

| Command | Reward | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `!daily` | Up to 100g | 24 hours |
| `!weekly` | Up to 1,000 XP + 7% chance at an uncommon or better item. | Resets every Monday at midnight UTC. |

> [!NOTE]
> The XP curve is inspired by the BECMI D&D ruleset — early levels come quickly, but the road to Level 40 is a long one.
