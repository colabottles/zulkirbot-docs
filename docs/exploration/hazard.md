# Environmental Hazards

During `!explore`, there is a 4% chance of triggering an environmental hazard. Hazards also fire as a penalty for failing riddles (3 wrong answers or timeout).

## Damage

Hazards deal **5–16 HP damage** with a random roll each time.

## Class Resistances

| Class | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Ranger, Druid, Barbarian | Immune — no damage taken |
| Fighter, Paladin, Monk | Resistant — half damage |
| All other classes | Full damage |

## Hazard Types

| Hazard | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Cave-In | The ceiling gives way without warning. |
| Poison Gas Vent | A crack in the floor releases a noxious cloud. |
| Collapsing Floor | The stonework crumbles beneath your feet. |
| Arcane Feedback | A residual spell discharge crackles through the air. |
| Flooding Corridor | Water surges from a hidden channel. |
| Falling Stalactite | Something shifts above and comes down fast. |
| Necrotic Seepage | The walls weep dark energy. It finds you. |
| Pressure Plate | A click underfoot. Then consequences. |
| Spore Cloud | A fungal colony detonates at your approach. |
| Unstable Rune | Someone's old ward activates. Incorrectly. |

## Death by Hazard

If a hazard reduces your HP to 0 or below, your character dies and is added to the graveyard. Use `!join` to start over.

## Immune Message Example

> ⚠️ @username — Cave-In! The ceiling gives way without warning. Your experience keeps you out of harm's way. No damage taken.

## Resistant Message Example

> ⚠️ @username — Poison Gas Vent! A crack in the floor releases a noxious cloud. 6 damage taken (halved — your training absorbs some of it). (HP: 44/50)
