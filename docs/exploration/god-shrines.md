# God Shrines

During `!explore`, there is a 4% chance of discovering a shrine to one of ten gods from the D&D pantheon. The shrine's effect depends on your class alignment and the god's alignment.

## The Gods

### Good Gods

| God | Domain | Boon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mystra | Magic | +5 ATK until next fight ends |
| Tempus | War | +5 DMG until next fight ends |
| Tyr | Justice | +5 DEF until next fight ends |
| Lathander | Dawn | +5 ATK until next fight ends |
| Selûne | Moon | +5 DEF until next fight ends |
| Oghma | Knowledge | +5 DMG until next fight ends |
| Kelemvor | Death | +5 DEF until next fight ends |

### Evil Gods

| God | Domain | Boon (evil classes) | Debuff (good classes) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shar | Darkness | +5 DMG | -5 DEF |
| Bane | Tyranny | +5 ATK | -5 ATK |
| Gruumsh | Slaughter | +5 DMG | -5 DMG |

## Class Alignment

**Good-aligned classes** receive boons from good gods and debuffs from evil gods:
Cleric, Paladin, Favored Soul, Sacred Fist, Ranger, Druid, Bard, Monk

**Evil-aligned classes** receive boons from evil gods and nothing from good gods:
Dark Apostate, Blightcaster, Acolyte of the Skin

**Neutral classes** receive nothing from either alignment — the shrine acknowledges your presence and moves on.

## Duration

All shrine effects last until your next fight ends, then clear automatically.
